# Third-party resources

Game code, geometric artwork, and synthesized sound effects were authored for this project.

- LibGDX 1.13.1 and gdx-jnigen-loader 2.5.2: Apache 2.0. Notice in `assets/licenses/LibGDX-LICENSE.txt`; source: https://github.com/libgdx/libgdx/tree/1.13.1
- LWJGL 3.3.3: BSD 3-clause. Notice in `assets/licenses/LWJGL-LICENSE.md`; source: https://github.com/LWJGL/lwjgl3/tree/3.3.3
- Native GLFW, jemalloc, libffi, Khronos bindings and OpenAL Soft: upstream notices in `assets/licenses/`. Unmodified binaries are supplied by LWJGL Maven dependencies. OpenAL Soft source: https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft
- Noto Sans KR: SIL Open Font License 1.1, `assets/licenses/NotoSansKR-OFL.txt`; source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanskr . Only a rendered subset atlas is distributed.
- JLayer 1.0.1-gdx and JOrbis 0.0.17 are transitive LibGDX audio dependencies (LGPL). Upstream: https://github.com/libgdx/jlayer-gdx and http://www.jcraft.com/jorbis/ . The game uses only original PCM WAV effects.
- Gradle wrapper 8.14.3: Apache 2.0. https://github.com/gradle/gradle
- The macOS application includes an Eclipse Temurin OpenJDK 17 runtime generated by jlink. Its notices are retained under `Contents/Resources/runtime/legal/`; source: https://github.com/adoptium/temurin17-binaries/releases/tag/jdk-17.0.15%2B6
